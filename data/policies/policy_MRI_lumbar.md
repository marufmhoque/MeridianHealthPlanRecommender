# Meridian Health Plan - Medical Policy MP-204
## Lumbar Spine MRI (CPT 72148, 72149, 72158)
Effective: 2026-01-01

### Prior authorization required: YES (all commercial and Medicare Advantage plans)

### Criteria - approve if ANY ONE of the following is met:
1. Red-flag symptoms: suspected cauda equina syndrome (saddle anesthesia, new bowel/bladder dysfunction), progressive neurological deficit, suspected infection (fever + IV drug use or immunosuppression), or history of cancer with new back pain.
2. Radiculopathy persisting at least 6 weeks DESPITE documented conservative therapy, where conservative therapy = at least 6 weeks of physical therapy OR a home exercise program supervised by a clinician, AND trial of NSAIDs or other analgesics (unless contraindicated).
3. Pre-surgical planning when a spine surgeon has documented that surgery is being considered.

### Not medically necessary:
- Uncomplicated low back pain under 6 weeks without red flags.
- Repeat imaging within 12 months without a documented change in clinical status.

### Required documentation:
- Office note dated within 30 days of request
- Duration of symptoms
- Conservative therapy type, dates, and response
- Neurological exam findings
