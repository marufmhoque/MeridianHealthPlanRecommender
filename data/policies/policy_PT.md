# Meridian Health Plan - Medical Policy MP-310
## Outpatient Physical Therapy (CPT 97110, 97140, 97530)
Effective: 2026-01-01

### Prior authorization required:
- Visits 1-12 per calendar year: NO authorization required.
- Visits 13+ per calendar year: authorization REQUIRED.

### Criteria for visits 13+:
1. Documented measurable functional goals.
2. Objective progress since the start of care (e.g., improved range of motion, strength grades, validated outcome score such as ODI or LEFS).
3. Expected continued improvement; maintenance-only therapy is not covered.

### Maximum: 30 visits per calendar year combined PT/OT, regardless of authorization.
